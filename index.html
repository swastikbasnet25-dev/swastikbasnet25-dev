[ A LITTLE ABOUT ME ]

CREATIVE BY
INSTINCT.
DESIGNER BY CHOICE.

I’m a tech-savvy and creative student who’s passionate about web development, software development, UI/UX design, and graphic design. I enjoy mixing technology with creativity and turning my ideas into designs and digital experiences that are both visually appealing and useful. I work with Photoshop, Illustrator, and Figma for design, while learning and experimenting with Java, C, HTML, and Git for development. I’m still learning and exploring, but I genuinely enjoy building things, trying out new ideas, and improving my skills along the way. I’m interested in growing as both a designer and developer and connecting with people who share the same passion for technology and creativity.
